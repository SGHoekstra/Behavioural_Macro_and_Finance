\subsection{Internal Analysis Optimal Consumption Agent-Based Model}
In this section we report the cross- and autocorrelations of the optimal consumption agent based model with conditional ARX expectations and unconditional expectations, all version of the extended heuristic agent based model, and all versions of the base agent based model.

\begin{figure}[h!]
    \centering
    \includesvg[width=\textwidth]{figs/optimal_consumption/calibrated/autocorr_abmx_uf.svg}
		\caption{Auto-correlations of quarterly real gross domestic product of of paths of variables the real quarterly gross domestic product, real quarterly fixed capital formation, real quarterly household consumption, and quarterly employment. The variables paths are generated by the unconditional ARX optimal consumption agent based model. Generated variable paths are first filtered with the Hodrick-Prescott filter. Monte Carlo sample paths are generated for 2010Q1:2011Q4 for 100 times. The blue line is the mean of the calculated auto-correlation whereas the blue error-bar is the standard deviation of the calculated paths. The red line is the auto-correlation observed in the data. }
    \label{fig:autocorr-oc-abmx-uf}
\end{figure}
